## TASK-UI-4 · Smart restaurant card

**Goal.** The pick opens into a detail page that tells the guest about the place and why Cravera picked it, from Google Places facts only. AGENTS.md's rule stands: the LLM never produces restaurant data.

**Review level (proposed):** L3 (new Places data, a new Gemini call fed with review text, attribution rules).

### Exit criteria (draft, for approval)

- [ ] The card opens into a scrollable detail page: a photo gallery from Places, up to 5 Places reviews each with its author's name, photo and link (Google's attribution rules), Google's `editorialSummary` and review summaries where available, and attributes (`servesVegetarianFood`, `outdoorSeating`, `takeout`, `delivery`, `servesBeer`/`servesWine`, `goodForGroups`, …).
- [ ] Menu: Places has no menu items, so the page links to the restaurant's `websiteUri` and its Google Maps page.
- [ ] "Why this pick": Gemini explains the match between the craving and the place using only the Places facts passed to it; each claim is tied to the field or review it came from, claims that can't be traced are dropped, and when Gemini fails the card shows today's Places-only reasons (Budget-friendly, Close by, Highly rated).
- [ ] Details and the Gemini call load when the card opens, not during search (search keeps its 11 s server budget).
- [ ] Tests for the traceability check and the fallback; checked in the app on Android and iPhone.

### Decisions needed before building

1. **An ADR on the LLM-data rule:** may Gemini summarize Places facts and reviews into sentences on screen, if each claim is traceable to a field? (ADR 0002 and AGENTS.md today: Gemini only turns a craving into filters and orders refs.)
2. **Places cost and quota:** reviews, attributes and summaries are higher SKUs (Enterprise / Enterprise + Atmosphere). This changes the free-quota and daily-quota plan in CURRENT.md's security gate.
3. **Review text to Gemini's free tier:** Google may use that content (the open free-vs-paid decision in CURRENT.md).
4. **Photos:** a gallery reverses the 2026-11-27 decision "keep photos free, first pick only".
